Video Games

7 Picks
NUMBER 1 BEST GAME OF ALL TIME GAME

Red Dead Redemption 2

Rockstar Games · 2018 · Open World Western

"Arthur Morgan's story, the atmospheric open world, and the insane level of detail set a standard nothing has topped."

GAME PEAK COMBAT

God of War & Ragnarök

Santa Monica Studio · 2018 / 2022

Kratos and Atreus across the Norse realms. The Leviathan Axe throw and recall mechanic never gets old.

GAME TIMELESS

Grand Theft Auto V

Rockstar Games · 2013

Over a decade later and Los Santos remains the benchmark for open-world sandbox design.

GAME CLASSIC

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Rockstar Games · 2004

Grove Street, CJ, turf wars, and the greatest map variety on the PS2 generation. Peak open-world atmosphere.

GAME PEAK FPS

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2

Infinity Ward · 2009

The golden era of arcade FPS. Iconic campaign missions, Rust 1v1s, and the most memorable multiplayer lobbies in gaming history.

GAME FIGHTING

Mortal Kombat 1

NetherRealm Studios · 2023

Fluid aerial combos, fresh Kameo fighter dynamics, and crisp visual presentation.

GAME REBOOT GOAT

Mortal Kombat 9

NetherRealm Studios · 2011

The game that saved Mortal Kombat. Dark tone, legendary roster, and the best story mode in fighting games.

Anime

2 Picks

Jujutsu Kaisen

MAPPA · 2020 · Dark Fantasy

Cursed energy, domain expansions, and fight choreography that goes harder than it has any right to. Gojo alone carries seasons.

Naruto

Studio Pierrot · 2002 · Shonen

Pain arc, the Itachi reveal, Rock Lee dropping the weights. Skip the filler and this is one of the best stories ever told in anime.

TV Shows

4 Picks

Moon Knight

Marvel Studios · 2022 · MCU

Oscar Isaac playing two people in the same body. The Egyptian mythology angle and the identity crisis made this the best Marvel show.

Breaking Bad + Better Call Saul

AMC · 2008 / 2015 · Crime Drama

Walter White's descent and Jimmy McGill's transformation. Two shows that are really one story, and the writing never dips once.

The Boys

Amazon Studios · 2019 · Superhero Satire

What happens when superheroes are corporate products. Homelander is the best villain on TV right now and it is not close.

Loki

Marvel Studios · 2021 · MCU

The TVA, variants, and time branching. Season 2's ending where he becomes the god of stories hit different.
